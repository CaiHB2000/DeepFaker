\section{Limitations}
Our method assumes moderately strong multimodal teachers and observable events with explicit IDs; when teachers are very weak or event structure is unavailable, gating and event-level reliability offer limited benefit and we fall back to instance-level gating only. Gating also trades coverage for precision, so rare positives in low-reliability events may be under-trained unless paired with positive-class costs or human review. Training cost grows with the number of teacher branches and auxiliary views, although inference uses only the student.

Finally, our main experiments focus on Weibo, WeFEND, and MiRAGeNews. On a strict time-split version of Fakeddit, all content-only baselines (including BERT, EANN, SAFE, SpotFake+, and our student) perform poorly and \modelname{} does not consistently outperform the teacher. We therefore treat strict Fakeddit and similar regimes with very weak teachers as hard cases rather than primary benchmarks. Broader multilingual or audio/video corpora, and extensions to single-head VLMs, remain future work.
