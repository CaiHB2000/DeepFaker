\section{Related Work}
\paragraph{Multimodal Misinformation Detection.}
Event-invariant representation learning improves topic robustness (EANN; \citealp{Wang2018EANN}). Subsequent work enhanced fusion and context modeling via similarity-aware architectures \citep{Zhou2020SAFE} and graph/social signals for explainability and early detection \citep{Lu2020GCAN,Nguyen2020FANG}. Data resources evolved from platform-specific rumor corpora to larger multimodal benchmarks such as Fakeddit \citep{Nakamura2020Fakeddit} and FakeNewsNet \citep{Shu2020FakeNewsNet}, enabling finer-grained evaluation. In parallel, vision--language pretraining (e.g., ALBEF/BLIP/BLIP-2) \citep{Li2021ALBEF,Li2022BLIP,Li2023BLIP2} and CLIP-style models \citep{Radford2021CLIP} improved cross-modal alignment and provide strong encoders for downstream detection. Our focus is orthogonal: rather than new backbones or graph modules, we study \emph{selective transfer} from imperfect teachers in a reliability-aware way.

\paragraph{Knowledge Distillation and Self-Training.}
KD compresses heavy models into compact students \citep{Hinton2015Distillation,Gou2021KDsurvey}; mutual/self-distillation can further enhance students \citep{Zhang2018DML,Furlanello2018BAN}. However, KD may propagate teacher mistakes under distribution shift. Robust training under label noise (e.g., Co-teaching and data-driven curricula) \citep{Han2018Coteaching,MentorNet2018} motivates \emph{selecting} reliable supervision. Self-training with Noisy Student \citep{Xie2020NoisyStudent} shows that pseudo-labels benefit from confidence control and strong augmentation---a perspective shared by our agreement- and reliability-gated transfer.

\paragraph{Uncertainty and Calibration.}
Modern networks are miscalibrated \citep{Guo2017Calibration}; temperature scaling is a strong post-hoc baseline. Evidential learning \citep{Sensoy2018EDL} and prior networks \citep{Malinin2018PN} provide principled uncertainty estimates. Label smoothing improves generalization and sometimes calibration \citep{Muller2019LabelSmoothing}. We integrate calibration into training via temperature-aware KL and evidential regularization, and explicitly target positive-class recall, complementing post-hoc scaling.

\paragraph{Recent Trends (2024--2025).}
Large vision--language models (VLMs) and multimodal LLMs are increasingly used as strong encoders or as teachers for downstream detection, often paired with retrieval or external tools to improve factual grounding. Synthetic data and instruction-style supervision have been explored to bridge distribution shift and expose rare phenomena, while new evaluations emphasize calibration, out-of-distribution robustness, and error analysis beyond single accuracy numbers. Our work is complementary: rather than relying on ever-larger teachers, we focus on \emph{selective, reliability-aware transfer} that improves a compact student under topic shift and noisy supervision, and we evaluate with calibration- and event-aware analyses.
